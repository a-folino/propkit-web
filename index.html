Quick FAQ

Is this financial, legal or tax advice?
No. PropKit provides estimates only and is not financial, legal or tax advice. Always confirm figures with official state revenue offices and licensed professionals.

Why might calculations differ from official government tools?
Thresholds, concessions and rates change over time. Make sure contract dates, buyer type, property type and values match official inputs.

Do I need an account?
No. The calculators work without an account. You only need a free account to save properties to My properties, so they're backed up and available on your other devices. Free accounts can save one property. PropKit Pro saves unlimited properties.

What does PropKit Pro include?
Equity projections for any property, unlimited saved properties, comparing properties side by side, portfolio projection and no ads. In Australia it's A$2.99 a month or A$19.99 a year. Prices in other countries are set by the App Store.

How do I delete my account?
In the app, go to Account and tap Delete account, then confirm twice. This permanently deletes your account and the properties you've saved. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a PropKit Pro subscription, so cancel that separately (see below). You can also email us and we'll delete your account for you.

How do I cancel PropKit Pro?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. In the app, open PropKit Pro and tap Manage subscription or go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

I bought Ad-Free or Pro but it isn't showing.
Open Account in the app and tap Restore purchases, making sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID you used to buy.

PropKit – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

PropKit is a property calculator app for Australia. We respect your privacy and collect as little data as we can. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us process it and the choices you have.

Data We Collect

  • Calculator inputs (e.g. prices, income, loan amounts) are processed on your device. They are not sent to us unless you choose to save a property.
  • Account details, if you create an account: your email address (or, with Sign in with Apple, the email Apple provides, which may be a private relay address) and an account ID.
  • Saved properties, if you save them: the details you enter, such as a property name, state, value, purchase price, land value, loan balance, interest rate, repayment type, rent and costs. These are stored on our servers so you can access them from your devices.
  • Purchase information: whether you have bought Ad-Free or subscribed to PropKit Pro, used to unlock those features and to support Restore Purchases. We never see your payment card details. Payments are handled by Apple.
  • Advertising data and device identifiers, which may be collected by our advertising partner when ads are shown.
  • App preferences stored on your device, such as your chosen state or territory.

How We Use Data

  • Calculator inputs are used only to perform calculations in the app.
  • Account details are used to sign you in and keep your saved properties private to you.
  • Saved properties are used only to show them back to you and calculate your portfolio figures.
  • Purchase data is used only to unlock Ad-Free and Pro features.
  • Advertising data is used solely for displaying and measuring ads.

Advertising & Tracking

PropKit displays third-party advertisements using Google AdMob.

  • PropKit requests non-personalised ads and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Our advertising partner may still collect device identifiers and advertising data to deliver ads, limit how often you see them and measure them.
  • If you purchase Ad-Free or subscribe to PropKit Pro, ads are turned off.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only with the service providers above, so they can provide their services to us or where required by law.

Security & Retention

  • Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit and each account can only access its own saved properties.
  • We keep your account and saved properties until you delete them. Deleting a property removes it straight away. Deleting your account removes your account and all your saved properties. Copies may remain in routine backups for a short period before they're overwritten.

Your Choices & Rights

  • You can use the calculators without an account.
  • You can view, edit or delete your saved properties in the app at any time.
  • You can delete your account in the app under Account → Delete account or by emailing us.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it by emailing us.
  • If you have a privacy concern, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Children's Privacy

PropKit is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date on this page.

Subscriptions & Terms

PropKit Pro

  • PropKit Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, available monthly or yearly. In Australia it's A$2.99 a month or A$19.99 a year. It unlocks equity projections, unlimited saved properties, comparing properties and portfolio projection. It also removes ads.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase.
  • Your subscription renews automatically at the same price for the same period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in the app (PropKit Pro → Manage subscription) or in your Apple ID settings. Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.
  • Deleting your PropKit account doesn't cancel your subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID settings.
  • Ad-Free is a one-off purchase that removes ads permanently.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under its refund policies.
